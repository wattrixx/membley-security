# Requirements

## Public Website
- Responsive homepage with hero carousel.
- College information and course listings.
- Security services information.
- About the group.
- Blog/news and announcements.
- Contact information and location/map.
- Contact and enquiry forms.

## Administration
- Secure administrator login.
- Dashboard.
- Manage pages.
- Manage courses and course categories.
- Manage blog posts and announcements.
- Manage media.
- Review applications and enquiries.
- Manage administrator accounts and permissions.

## Important Business Rules
- College applications and security enquiries must be kept separate.
- Course fees and enrolment availability must be confirmed before publication.
- Draft and unpublished content must not appear publicly.
- Administrative access must be permission-based.
- Internal planning information must not be published.
